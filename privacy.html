VSolitaire 13 yaş ve üzeri kullanıcılar için tasarlanmıştır ve 13 yaş altındaki çocuklardan bilerek kişisel veri toplamaz.

VSolitaire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 • Developer: Volkan Şentürk

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

VSolitaire stores your game statistics, selected theme, saved game progress, and VIP status locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data on external developer servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

VSolitaire uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google AdMob may process your device Advertising ID and basic diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-App Purchases

All VIP subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. We never access or store your payment details.

4. Children's Privacy

VSolitaire is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
