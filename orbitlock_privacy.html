English

OrbitLock - Ring Puzzle respects your privacy. Your game progress, settings, and high scores are stored locally on your device and optionally synced via Google Play Games Services / Apple Game Center.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): In the free version, ads may be displayed. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Purchasing the Remove Ads / VIP pack permanently disables interstitial ads.
  • In-App Purchases: All purchases are processed securely by Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not collect or store payment information.
